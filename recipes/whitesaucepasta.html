White Sauce Pasta

Home

One of the many amazing ways to incorporate white sauce into a really delicious meal is to make White Sauce Pasta. This dish offers a well-rounded meal that is satisfying and a great way to incorporate vegetables into a pasta that will please your family. It is customizable by changing the vegetables or even the pasta used. The backbone of this recipe is the sauce, so getting it right is really the key. It is thickened with a mixture of butter and flour in equal amounts which is called roux, in French. The method is rather simple, but must be followed properly for a smooth, creamy and velvety sauce. The flexibility that bechamel sauce offers makes it a perfect candidate for adjusting it to your liking. It really is a blank slate for a wide range of seasonings. Fresh or dried herbs like thyme, oregano and basil bring a not just fresh flavor, but also a fantastic aroma. Use a variety of veggies like carrots, broccoli, peas, corn, bell peppers to enhance the nutrition & for a beautiful color. or Serve it plain with a side of roasted veggies, meat or sea food. It goes perfectly well with anything!

The required ingredients are:
  • Pasta
  • Butter
  • All-purpose flour
  • Milk
  • Veggies
  • Garlic
  • Cheese
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • black pepper
  • chili flakes
  • oregano

Steps :

  1. Melt butter and margarine in a medium saucepan over medium-low heat
  2. Add flour and crumbled bouillon; stir until well blended, 1 to 2 minutes
  3. Continue to cook and stir until thickened and lightly browned, about 5 minutes.
  4. Increase heat to medium and whisk in boiling water until smooth.
  5. Combine ricotta cheese, grated Parmesan cheese, and shredded mozzarella cheese in a bowl.
  6. Stir in milk; cook and stir until hot and thickened, 2 to 3 more minutes.
  7. Season with white pepper.
  8. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and stir until evenly coated.
  9. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pasta is heated through.
  10. Season with additional salt and white pepper to taste.
  11. Add grated Parmesan cheese and stir until melted and well combined.
  12. Garnish with chopped parsley and a little extra Parmesan cheese.
  13. Serve hot and enjoy.
