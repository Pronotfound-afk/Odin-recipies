Lasagna, alio known by the plural form lasagne, is a type of pasta made in wide, flat sheets.It originates in Italian cuisine, where it is served in a number of ways, but is most often served as a casserole made by stacked layers of pasta, alternating with fillings that could consist of meat, dairy, vegetables, and sauces. Typically, cooked pasta is assembled with the aforementioned fillings, topped with grated cheese, and then baked in an oven (al forno). Regional variations of this are found across Italy,along with Americanized versions worldwide.

As with most other types of pasta, the Italian word is a plural form: lasagne (Italian: [laˈzaɲɲe]) meaning more than one sheet of lasagna (UK: /ləˈzænjə/,[4] US: /ləˈzɑːnjə/; Italian: [laˈzaɲɲa]). When referring to the baked dish, regional usage in Italy favours the plural form lasagne in the north of the country and the singular lasagna in the south. The former plural usage has influenced the usual spelling found in British English, while the southern Italian singular usage has influenced the spelling often used in American English.[5] Both lasagna and lasagne are used as singular non-count (uncountable) nouns in English

The required ingredients are:
  • Ground meat
  • Aromatics
  • Tomatoes
  • Olive oil
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • dried oregano
  • Noodles
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Binder

Steps :

  1. Take ground beef and Italian sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add chopped onions and minced garlic, cooking until the onions are soft.
  3. Stir in marinara or crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, and seasonings like basil, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  4. Simmer the sauce on low heat for 10 to 30 minutes to thicken the flavors.
  5. Combine ricotta cheese, grated Parmesan cheese, and shredded mozzarella cheese in a bowl.
  6. Stir in eggs and fresh parsley or basil with a pinch of salt and pepper until smooth.
  7. Boil a large pot of salted water.
  8. Add lasagna noodles and cook until al dente (tender yet firm to the bite), usually a minute or two under package instructions.
  9. Drain the noodles and rinse them with cold water to stop the cooking process.
  10. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish to prevent the noodles from sticking.
  11. Place a layer of cooked lasagna noodles side-by-side.
  12. Spread a layer of the ricotta cheese mixture over the noodles.
  13. Spoon more meat sauce and an extra sprinkle of mozzarella over the cheese.
  14. Repeat these layers until you run out of ingredients, finishing the top layer with noodles, remaining meat sauce, and a heavy topping of mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  15. Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil.
  16. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 30 to 35 minutes.
  17. Remove the foil and bake for another 10 minutes until the cheese is melted, golden brown, and bubbling.
  18. Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing so the layers hold their shape.
